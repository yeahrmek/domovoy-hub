# Domovoy Hub

*A domovoy (домовой) is the house spirit of Slavic folklore — the quiet keeper
of the home who watches the door and keeps everything in order. This one runs
on a tablet in your hallway.*

Domovoy Hub is a single Android wall-panel app for flats where the smart home
grew organically: a couple of Yandex devices, some Aqara sensors, a Tuya/Smart
Life socket, a Xiaomi vacuum — each locked inside its own vendor app. Domovoy
Hub puts all of them behind one always-on screen next to the front door.

Most importantly, it answers the door: incoming calls from the **Domonap**
intercom are surfaced full-screen on the tablet, so you can see and buzz in
your visitor without hunting for a phone.
